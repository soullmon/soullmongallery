Soullmon

Soullmon (b. 2001) is a contemporary artist based in London, known for her dynamic approach to Abstract Expressionism. Her work challenges the boundaries between digital precision and organic chaos, often utilizing mixed media to create textured, immersive soundscapes on canvas.

Graduating with distinction from the Royal College of Art, Cole has spent the last decade refining a visual language that speaks to the modern condition—fragmented, vibrant, and relentlessly moving. Her series "Neon Genesis" was critically acclaimed for its bold use of color theory to evoke emotional resonance.

Her works are held in private collections across New York, Tokyo, and Berlin. Currently, she is exploring the intersection of AI generation and traditional oil painting techniques.
